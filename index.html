04 / THE DETAILS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

A little clarity before you choose.

What’s the difference between the plans?+

Core gives you the CRM without SMS. Starter, Growth, and Scale add TextVolt messaging, one SMS number, and SMS setup support. The main difference between the messaging plans is the monthly allowance: 5,000, 25,000, or 50,000 SMS.

Does the monthly price include the CRM and texting?+

Yes, for Starter, Growth, and Scale. Each bundles Flow Fusion CRM, the listed SMS allowance, and one SMS number into the monthly plan price. Core does not include SMS. Additional SMS numbers are $5 each per month.

What is TextVolt?+

TextVolt is the SMS provider that connects to Flow Fusion so you can send and receive text messages inside your CRM. It handles messaging, not phone calls.

Do incoming messages count toward my allowance?+

Yes. Your monthly SMS allowance covers both sent and received messages. It is not a separate allowance for each direction.

Can I make phone calls with my included number?+

The included TextVolt number is for SMS. Calling requires a separate calling service and number. Voice usage is not part of the included SMS allowance.

What should I set up first?+

Start with your contacts, pipeline, and one follow-up workflow. Add your calendar so interested leads have a clear next step. If you choose an SMS plan, texting setup support is included.
